### 小说原文

```text
"Honestly? Breaking up with Chloe was a favor. She's almost thirty — I was getting bored. Now I can find someone young and hot. Plenty of fish in the sea!"

Dive bar, DTLA. Luke Lawson gripped his beer bottle and grinned at Marcus Cole.

Marcus raised an eyebrow. "You mean that?"

"Dead serious. You know what marriage cost? Her mom's list: a million-dollar house, a hundred-fifty-K car, a two-hundred-K wedding. My parents sold our place in Ohio. Borrowed on top of that. I drained five years of savings. Screw the wedding!"

"I'd want him too, if I were a woman."

Marcus said nothing. Just refilled the glass.

"Marcus... six years. Whatever she wanted, I gave. What's wrong with me?" The grin cracked. Tears hit the beer. "The iPhone, the bags, the new car — all hers. I never even touched her! And she got into his car, in front of everyone, straight to a hotel!"

He wiped his face and laughed through it. "Why can't this two hundred grand turn into two trillion?!"

He raised the bottle at the ceiling and roared: "God — what did I ever do to you?!"

Outside, rain slammed down.

His apartment: roses in a heart shape, balloons on the ceiling, a diamond ring on the table. He stared for a long moment. Then the roses went into the trash, the balloons popped one by one. He pocketed the ring, hands shaking.

"Tomorrow — I get my condo keys and car keys back."

His phone buzzed: Payment due tomorrow: $1,699.37.

*The wedding money covers the debt. Mom and Dad... not tonight.*

He fell asleep in his clothes. Rain drummed all night.

Morning. Sunlight stabbed his eyes. His phone exploded with alerts.

"Downtown LA condos: 650,000 mils a unit — buyers fighting over them!"

"the World Wealth Index ranked, Jeff Bexon — worth just $20,000. A twenty-thousandaire."

"And Bexon claims the true richest man is someone else — unknown."

Luke sat up, scratching his head, and burst out laughing. "Bexon? Twenty grand? That's 'richest'?"

*If twenty grand is the 'richest man'... my two hundred grand makes me a GOD.*

He opened a shopping app, half-believing. A mansion: one dollar.

"A mansion... for ONE dollar?" He stared at the price tag. Wanted to believe it. Didn't dare. He snorted, tossed the phone back on the bed, and shuffled to the bathroom.

He didn't know yet — every word of that news was true.

Overnight, global prices had fallen ten million to one. 1 USD = 10,000,000 mils. Only HIS balance — unchanged.
```

## 剧集 E02｜一套豪宅一美金

### 小说原文

```text
Morning. Luke brushed his teeth, eyes bloodshot. His phone lit up: an Buzz Story — a hotel bed, Preston's watch resting on Chloe's shoulder. Caption: "Meeting the right person showed me everything before was just a mistake."

The toothbrush stopped. His knuckles went white around the rinsing cup.

*A mistake? Six YEARS was a mistake?*

After a long moment he put the phone down and pulled on his gym polo.

*He could crush me with his pocket change. I'm nobody. Just a broke trainer.*

The door slammed behind him.

Corner diner, steam rising. A chalkboard menu: Breakfast combo — 10 mils.

"One combo. Cash." He pulled out a ten-dollar bill — the one he'd fished off the pavement last night.

The owner took it, froze, squinted, stepped back two paces, and slammed the counter. "Is this a joke?! That's FAKE money! I'm calling the cops!"

"That's a real bill. Every fiber of it."

"REAL?! I've only seen a ten-dollar BILL on TV, son! You, a broke trainer eating a ten-mil breakfast, pulling a TEN?!"

"Ten mils? Isn't breakfast ten dollars?"

"You think my eggs are made of gold? Read my sign! One dollar equals TEN MILLION mils! Ten bucks buys this whole block!"

The whole diner went quiet. Customers pulled out their phones to check the menu — then all stared at Luke like he was insane.

His hands trembling, Luke opened a shopping app. iPhone: 1,000 mils. Ferrari: 5,000,000 mils. DTLA Mansion: $1.

"A mansion... for ONE dollar?!"

His bank alert: Auto-payment 1,699.37 mils processed. Balance: $211,436, plus 9,999,830 mils.

*The world changed overnight. Except my money. Two hundred thousand mansions. Four hundred thousand Ferraris...*

*I'm officially richer than the richest man on Earth.*

He covered his mouth, swallowed the joy, scanned and paid — the screen showed Paid: 10 mils. The owner smirked. "Stick with me, kid. I didn't get this smart by accident."

Luke pocketed the bill and scanned the street, careful.

*Never flash real money in public. That's how people get robbed.*

A black Audi pulled up. A stunning woman stepped out, hands folded, head slightly down, walking straight toward him.

"Excuse me, sir. My name is Serena. Would you... spare two minutes of your time?"

Onlookers raised their phones. Luke blinked at her, lost.
```

### 小说原文

```text
Serena's eyes dropped to the shoe rack — a dozen one-dollar coins, gold rims catching morning light, one buried under a sock. Then her gaze slid to the coffee table where Luke had tossed his wallet.

A card slipped halfway out of the leather fold. Black-purple surface. A gold eagle emblem. Two lines of embossed text: GLOBAL BANKING ALLIANCE.

Serena's breath caught.

"That's... the black-gold card?! Grandpa said it was real?!"

The legendary card. Nobody knew who held one. Nobody knew how many existed. Unlimited global transfers. Total anonymity — even the Alliance's own employees couldn't look up the cardholder. And it was sitting on a coffee table next to a sock.

Luke looked up from his water glass. "What? Is something wrong?"

Serena shot to her feet and bowed at a perfect ninety degrees. "I apologize! I spoke out of turn. You obviously know what this card is — I never should have—"

"Sit down. I honestly don't. But the card — I'll activate it. And the money — I'll deposit."

He said it like he was ordering lunch. Serena's hands trembled as she pulled out her business card and handed it over with both hands.

"Meridian Bank would be honored to manage your portfolio. Full private banking. Dedicated advisor. Global liquid access. Three-year CD at ten percent."

Luke nodded, fingers tapping the coffee table like he was appraising a used couch. "Fine. And I want a second card. Can't exactly flash the black one at a grocery store."

He said it like a man who had ten black-gold cards at home. Serena's heart raced.

*This is why I chose this branch. This is why I fought Grandpa for the downtown territory. This man — Lawson — is my ticket out of that marriage.*

The door handle turned.

Chloe walked in.
```
